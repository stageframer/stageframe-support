StageFrame Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

StageFrame is a local-first production planning application for theatre makers. This policy explains how StageFrame handles information.

Information stored by StageFrame

StageFrame stores the productions and related information you create or import locally on your device. This may include scripts, notes, character and cast information, rehearsal information, production plans, and other content you choose to enter.

StageFrame does not require an account and does not upload this production information to a StageFrame-operated server.

Files you import and export

When you choose to import a file, StageFrame accesses that file only to perform the requested import. When you export a StageFrame production, you choose where the exported file is saved or shared. StageFrame does not independently transmit imported or exported files to the developer.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

StageFrame does not include:

  • Advertising
  • Third-party analytics SDKs
  • Cross-app or cross-website tracking
  • Behavioral profiling
  • Sale of personal information

StageFrame does not collect product-usage analytics or production content on behalf of the developer.

Apple services

If you install StageFrame through the App Store or TestFlight, Apple may process download, usage, diagnostic, crash, and feedback information according to your device settings and Apple's own policies. Information made available to the developer through Apple's services is governed by Apple's privacy practices.

You can learn more in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Feedback and support

If you contact the developer by email, the developer will receive your email address and any information you choose to include. This information is used only to respond to your message, investigate an issue, or improve StageFrame.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below.

Deleting your information

Because StageFrame stores production information locally, you control that information through the app and your device. You can delete productions within StageFrame. Deleting StageFrame from your device also removes locally stored app data, subject to any device backups or exported files you created.

Exported files and device backups are controlled by you and by the services where you choose to store them.

Children's privacy

StageFrame is not designed to collect personal information from children, and the developer does not knowingly collect such information through the app.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if StageFrame's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or deletion requests, contact: stageframeapp@gmail.com
